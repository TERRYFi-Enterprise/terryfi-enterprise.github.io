The problem

Too much of the bank still runs on people moving data by hand.

Efficiency ratios above peers

Many small and regional banks spend well more per dollar of revenue than the median bank their size. Every point above peer is cost that comes straight out of earnings.

Manual operations

Loan review in spreadsheets, re-keying between systems, reconciliations, exception queues and report assembly eat staff hours every day.

Projects that never land

New software often means a long implementation, a new system to staff, and workflows that still route around it. The cost stays where it was.

How it works

Engineers who work inside your operation, not around it.

  1. 01

    Embed with your ops team

    Our engineers sit with the people doing the work and map how each process actually runs today.

  2. 02

    Find the manual work

    We measure where the hours go and pick the workflows where automation lowers cost the most, with your control owners in the loop.

  3. 03

    Automate on your existing systems

    We build on your current core and tools wherever practical. No rip-and-replace, and every change goes through your review and sign-off.

  4. 04

    Stay and keep improving

    We measure the result, keep the systems running, and move to the next workflow. The work compounds instead of ending at go-live.

Who it's for

Small and regional banks, starting in Florida and the Southeast.

We work with community and regional banks whose leadership wants a lower cost to operate and more capacity from the team they already have. It's a fit if:

  • Your efficiency ratio sits above the median for banks your size.
  • Key work still depends on spreadsheets, re-keying or manual review.
